Quick 4-Step Firebase Setup Guide

1
Open Firebase Console: Go to console.firebase.google.com and click "Create a project" (or select an existing one).
2
Add a Web App: In Project Overview, click the Web icon (</>). Give your app a name and click "Register app". Copy the firebaseConfig values into the form above.
3
Enable Cloud Firestore Database: In the left sidebar, click "Build" > "Firestore Database", then click "Create database". Select "Start in test mode" (allows public read/write during development) and choose your preferred location.
4
Save & Push Data: Toggle "Enable Cloud Storage", click "Save Firebase Credentials", then click "Push to Firebase" to upload all your current settings to the cloud!
